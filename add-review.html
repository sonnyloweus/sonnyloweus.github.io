Quick fill from Google or Apple Maps
Google: use the full address-bar URL (starts with google.com/maps/place/...), not a share/short link — if you have a maps.app.goo.gl link, open it once in your browser first, then paste the full URL from the address bar.

Apple: tap Share on the place in Maps and paste the maps.apple.com link — those already carry name, coordinates, and address, so they work as-is.
